What your team gets

The parts of a file drive that small teams actually use, without the clutter.

Folders only the right people see
Restrict a client folder to the people working on it. Everyone else on the team won't know it's there.
Every version kept
Upload a file with the same name and the old one is saved as a version. Download or restore any of them.
Links you stay in charge of
Share a file with a password and an expiry date. See how often it was downloaded, and revoke it any time.
Preview without downloading
Open images, PDFs, video, audio and text right in the browser.
Whole folders in and out
Drag in a folder with everything inside it, or download any folder as a zip.
A record of who did what
Admins can see sign-ins, uploads, downloads, deletes and shares, and export them to a spreadsheet.
A Trash that forgives
Deleted files go to Trash first. Even after they're deleted for good, we can recover them for 30 days if you ask.
Works on any device
Nothing to install. TeamDrive runs in the browser on computers, tablets and phones.

Private by design

Run by Equawire in Ontario, Canada. Here's exactly what that means for your files. The details are in our Privacy Policy.

  • No ads, no tracking, no AI trainingWe don't sell data, use analytics cookies, or train AI on your files.
  • Your team is walled offEach company gets its own separate space. Other teams can't see anything in yours.
  • Two-step login, required for adminsAdmins always sign in with a code from an authenticator app. Everyone else can turn it on in one minute, with backup codes in case a phone is lost.
  • Encrypted in transit and at restFiles travel over HTTPS and are stored encrypted by Cloudflare, an established cloud storage provider.
  • You own your filesIf you leave, you get 30 days to download everything before it's deleted.
  • Clear about where data livesEquawire is based in Ontario, but our hosting and storage providers may keep data outside Canada, such as in the United States. If your files must stay in Canada, tell us before you start.

Questions

How much can we store?

Each team has a storage limit that we agree with you, and you can see how much you've used at the top of TeamDrive. Single files can be up to 2 GB. If you need more room, ask and we'll raise the limit.

Can we move over from Google Drive or Dropbox?

Yes. Download your folders from Google Drive (or use Google Takeout) or Dropbox, then drag the folders into TeamDrive. The folder structure is kept as it is.

Where are our files stored?

Files are stored by Cloudflare and the app runs on Hostinger. Both may keep data outside Canada, for example in the United States. Our Privacy Policy lists exactly what we store and for how long.

What happens to our share links if we leave?

They stop working when your team is closed. You get 30 days to download everything first, and then your files are deleted.

Do people need to install anything?

No. TeamDrive runs in any modern browser on computers, tablets and phones. Two-step login uses a free authenticator app such as Google Authenticator.

Can people outside our team get files?

Only through a share link you create. You can add a password and an expiry date, see how many times it was downloaded, and turn it off at any time.
