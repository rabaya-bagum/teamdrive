Your copy, your data

Your TeamDrive isn't shared with other companies. It's your own installation, running where you choose.

  • Only your company on itYour copy has its own address, database and file storage. No other customer's data lives alongside yours.
  • You choose where it runsWe can host it for you in Canada (Toronto), or install it on your own server or cloud account so it never leaves your hands.
  • No ads, no tracking, no AI trainingTeamDrive has no advertising, analytics or tracking built in, and your files are never used to train AI.
  • Two-step login, required for adminsAdmins always sign in with a code from an authenticator app. Everyone else can turn it on in one minute, with backup codes in case a phone is lost.
  • Encrypted in transit and at restFiles travel over HTTPS and are stored encrypted by the storage provider your copy uses.
  • You own it allIf you stop using our hosting, you can download everything or we'll move your copy to your own server.

Questions

How is the demo different from our own copy?

The demo is one shared account that anyone can try. Other visitors can see what you upload, files are limited to 2 MB, share links and account settings are off, and everything is reset every night. Your own copy is private to your company, with every feature turned on.

Where is our data stored?

Wherever your copy runs. If we host it, we keep it in Canada (Toronto). If it runs on your own server, it stays wherever that server is. The public demo runs in the United States, on servers in North Carolina with files in Cloudflare's Eastern North America region.

Who can see our files?

Only your team. If we host your copy, we look at it only to keep it running, when you ask us to help, or if the law requires it. If it runs on your own server, only you have access.

Can we move over from Google Drive or Dropbox?

Yes. Download your folders from Google Drive (or use Google Takeout) or Dropbox, then drag the folders into TeamDrive. The folder structure is kept as it is, and we can help with the move.

How much can we store?

As much as you need. Storage is sized for your team when we set up your copy, and single files can be up to 2 GB.

What if we stop using your hosting?

You can download everything, or we can move your copy to your own server so your team carries on as before.

Do people need to install anything?

No. TeamDrive runs in any modern browser on computers, tablets and phones. Two-step login uses a free authenticator app such as Google Authenticator.
